# 04 · Use your whole plan

Paid Claude plans have usage limits that reset on a schedule: a short window of a few hours,
and a weekly limit. **Weekly usage you don't spend before the reset is gone.** It's easy to
let a chunk of it expire without noticing.

The quota maximizer finds that spare usage and spends it on your most valuable work.

## How to use it

Ask, ideally a day or two before your reset:
- "How much of my plan is left?"
- "My limit resets soon, what should I do?"
- "Don't let me waste my plan this week."

## Step 1: find your numbers

| App | Where |
|---|---|
| claude.ai and the desktop app | **Settings → Usage** |
| Claude Code | Type `/usage` |

Paste a screenshot of the page (easiest), or tell Claude:
- Weekly usage % used
- The strongest model's % used, if shown separately
- When the weekly limit resets

Always read the reset date and time from that page. Don't rely on memory.

## Step 2: the verdict

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **Spend now** | 20% or more left, and the reset is within 2 days |
| **Spend tomorrow** | 40% or more left, and the reset is 2–3 days away |
| **Running hot** | The strongest model is 70%+ used with 3+ days left. Slow down or risk being locked out |
| **Nothing to do** | You're on pace |

## Step 3: the spend list

The kit asks which projects are active and builds a short list, best first. It prefers work
that's normally too expensive to do:

| Kind | Example |
|---|---|
| **Make it repeatable** | "I write a weekly report every Friday. Turn it into a checklist Claude can follow every time." |
| **Expert team review** | "Have specialists and two critics review my new sales page before launch." |
| **Deep check** | "I think most refunds come from one product. Check that against the full order export." |
| **Get ahead** | "Draft next week's newsletter and three social posts." |

## Step 4: spend it safely

- **One item at a time**, checking usage between items.
- **Keep 10% spare** for emergencies before the reset: stop starting new items at 90% used. Hitting 100% can lock you out until the reset.
- **Lower effort for most items**, so the spare covers more work.
- **Nothing outside happens without your OK:** no sending, posting, buying or deleting.
- **An item only counts as done when its check passes.**

## Make it a habit

Put a weekly reminder in your calendar for the day before your reset:
**"Ask Claude: how much of my plan is left?"** Ten seconds, every week, and your plan
works as hard as you do.
